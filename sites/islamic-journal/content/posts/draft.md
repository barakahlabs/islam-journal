The universe and all we perceive is either independent or dependent on something else dependant or dependent on something that is independant and eternal. "dependent on something else dependant" this is not possible hence conclusion that everything is dependent on something that is independent and eternal. Wajib al Wujood (Necessary existence) Mumkin al Wujood (Contingent existence)

Necessary: It means it was impossible for it to not have existed.
What is dependency?
1. It is not necessary: If one goes out for shopping and on the way he finds a block of cash, he would not assume that it was necessary that the cash would have existed there. It was possible to exist at the same time it was possible to not exist. It doesn't its existence itself.
2. Fundamental building blocks of a particular thing could have been arranged in a different way: You walk through a garden and see a bunch of flowers arranged in a specific way. It was possible that the flowers were arranged in a different way. By virtue, this means there must be an external explanation or external cause for it to have happened that way.
3. That particuar thing relies on something that is outside of its existence to exists: We require food and water for our existence.
4. Limited physical qualities: a Mobile phone has specific color, shape, configuration etc. Why does it have to have a specific color, shape, configuration etc.? It requires and external explantion on manufacturer decisions. It could have been in another way. Those limitation doesn't arise from themselves.

Apply this to universe. Implies they doesn't necessarily have to exist in the same way they are perceived by us.

Now if this universe was dependent on another dependant entity that would not give an ultimate explanation. That will lead to infinite regress.

This leads to the conclusion that the universe and everything we perceive are dependent on an external entity that exist by its own nature and is eternal and independant.

What if someone claims universe is dependant and also eternal? This is absurd and contradictory. Let's look into the argument by considering the universe is eternal. How is now that it is necessary and require no explanation? By virtue of those things that it is composed of being dependant, the universe doesn't necessarily have to exist  and hence not eternal

the only explantion is the universe is dependant on an eternal and independent entity.

Hence a being must be self-sufficient or dependent and every contingent being must be dependent on an eternal and independent entity.

Contentions:
1. What if science eventually finds an answer?
Answer: Any theory or conclusion from science can only refer to contingent entities and phenomena. Science cannot produce anything that is necessary existing or that is independent. Elliot Sober in his essay empericism states scientists are limited to observation at hand at any point in time.
2. Fallacy of composition: Fallacy of reasoning mistakenly concludes the whole must have same property as individual parts.
Answer:  This is a misplaced fallacy. We cannot say persian rug is light weight because individual threads are lightweight. That would be fallacy. But it can be the case, example a brick and a brick wall both are hard and strong. Universe is like brick wall because of dependency. Dependant parts always make dependant wholes.
